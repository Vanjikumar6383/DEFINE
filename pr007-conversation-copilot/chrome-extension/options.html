🌐 Supported Meeting Platforms

CONSENT-FIRST

Nimad Copilot is designed to complement existing web meeting platforms safely without unauthorized screen scraping or secret recording.

🟢 Google Meet

1. Open Google Meet in any Chrome tab.
2. During or after the meeting, copy transcript notes or live captions.
3. Open Nimad Copilot popup (or right-click selected text → Analyze with Nimad Copilot).
4. Instant AI replies and executive takeaways are produced immediately.

🔵 Zoom Web Meetings

1. Open Zoom in Chrome browser.
2. Export or copy meeting transcript text or key attendee dialogue.
3. Paste into Nimad Copilot popup to run full objective-aware analysis.

🟣 Microsoft Teams Web

1. In Teams meeting, open the Meeting Recap or live transcript feed.
2. Copy discussion points and paste directly into the Nimad Copilot popup.

🛡️ Privacy & Architecture Disclosures

Manifest V3 Compliance:

Strictly uses modern Chrome Manifest V3 APIs with minimal required permissions (storage, contextMenus). No unnecessary tab recording or secret background monitoring.

Zero Stored Secrets:

AI provider keys (Groq, OpenAI, ElevenLabs) and database credentials remain exclusively on your secure Nimad backend. The browser extension only holds a short-lived user JWT token.

Future Context Capture Roadmap:

Future automated meeting caption capture will strictly utilize official Google Workspace, Zoom Developer, and Microsoft Graph APIs with clear, explicit attendee consent banners.
